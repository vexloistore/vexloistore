Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

Vexloi LLC collects personal and corporate information when you interact with our website, request wholesale quotes, or place wholesale orders. This includes contact details, company names, TAX ID/EIN numbers, shipping addresses, and transaction histories.

2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize corporate data solely to process wholesale orders, generate Proforma Invoices, facilitate freight forwarder logistics out of Miami, FL, and communicate B2B pricing updates or order statuses.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell or rent commercial partner data. Information is shared strictly with necessary financial gateways (e.g., Wire/ACH processing partners) and logistics providers to fulfill distribution orders under strict security protocols.

4. Contact Information

For inquiries regarding data protection or corporate privacy practices, contact us at sales@vexloi.com or via our Miami, FL headquarters.
