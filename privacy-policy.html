Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Vexloi LLC collects personal and company information when you browse our catalog, request quotes or place wholesale orders. This includes contact details, company name, Tax ID/EIN, shipping and freight forwarder addresses, and order history.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information only to process wholesale orders, issue proforma invoices, coordinate export logistics from Miami, FL, and send order updates or B2B pricing information you request.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell or rent partner data. Information is shared only with payment processors and logistics providers needed to complete your order, under appropriate security measures.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website stores your cart and account session locally in your browser to keep your order between visits. You can clear this data at any time from your browser settings.

5. Contact

For privacy questions contact us at sales@vexloi.com.
