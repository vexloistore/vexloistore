Accessibility Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Our Commitment

Vexloi LLC is committed to making its digital catalog accessible to all visitors and business partners, following WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines where possible.

Continuous Improvement

We regularly review our pages, mobile layouts, color contrast and keyboard navigation to improve compatibility with assistive technologies and screen readers.

Assistance

If you have difficulty viewing product information, requesting a proforma invoice or navigating the site, email sales@vexloi.com and we will assist you promptly.
