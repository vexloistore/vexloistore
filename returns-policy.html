Returns & Warranty Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Manufacturer Warranty

All products are new, original and covered by the manufacturer's warranty according to each brand's terms. Warranty does not cover misuse, physical damage, unauthorized repairs or normal wear. Proof of purchase is required.

2. Dead on Arrival (DOA)

Units that fail on first power-up must be reported within 7 days of delivery with the serial number, a description of the issue and photos or video. We will coordinate replacement, credit or the brand's RMA process.

3. Wholesale Returns

Returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery only for unopened, factory-sealed units in original packaging. Opened electronics, software, licenses and special-order items are not returnable unless defective.

4. Damaged or Short Shipments

Report transit damage or quantity discrepancies within 48 hours of receipt, with photos of the packaging and pallets, before signing for the delivery whenever possible.

5. Return Authorization (RMA)

All returns require an RMA number issued by our sales team. Returns received without an RMA will not be processed.
